# Vocab Curve Studio V47 Guide

## Theme selection

Open **Settings → Appearance & power** and choose:

- **Follow system** — matches the operating-system appearance.
- **Light** — always uses the complete light theme.
- **Dark** — always uses the complete dark theme across every view, disclosure, dialog, sidebar, review surface, and phone destination.

The preference is applied before the main interface paints and persists after reload.

## Power modes

- **Full visuals** — all normal visual effects.
- **Low Power** — pauses continuous decorative motion and expensive blur while retaining short feedback.
- **Ultra Battery** — disables all CSS animation and transitions, stops liquid and memory-world controllers, removes ripple effects, and simplifies expensive visual layers. Study, ratings, scheduling, saving, Books, Stats, imports, and Pro Tutor remain operational.

## Revealed flashcard

Reveal no longer creates a separate rating panel below a narrower card. The flashcard retains its width, and the answer surface contains:

- Meaning
- Memory bridge
- Context and review information
- Wrong, Correct, and Know

On phones, the same controls remain inside the answer card, with additional context available through the contained disclosure when space is limited.

## Phone navigation

The fixed phone navigation exposes:

- Study
- Books
- More

Planner, Stats, and Settings remain available through More. The selection lens aligns with the visible destination.

## Existing study behavior

V47 retains the audited V46 scheduler, including mixed review/new study, review-only mode, recent-card spacing, due/early/reinforcement labels, day-scoped short-term evidence, recall calibration, and protected saves.
